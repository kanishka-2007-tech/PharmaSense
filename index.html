The Problem

In India and globally, a huge number of adverse drug reactions happen because patients — especially elderly ones on multiple medications — don't know which drugs interact badly, or misread doctor handwriting on prescriptions. Pharmacists catch some, but a lot slips through, especially in rural areas with fewer pharmacists per capita.

What This Does

PharmaSense checks a list of medicines against known drug-drug interactions and explains why each one matters, in plain language — not just a "contraindicated" flag. It flags duplicate therapy, shows severity stamps, and gives actionable recommendations.

Disclaimer

This is a portfolio demonstration built on a small curated dataset. It does not cover every medicine or interaction and is not a substitute for a pharmacist or doctor. Always confirm with a qualified professional.
